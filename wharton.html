Flagship Competition

Wharton Investment Competition

One of the world's most prestigious high-school investment competitions, organized by the Wharton School of Business at the University of Pennsylvania.

Why It Matters

Key Highlights

🌍

Global Recognition

Highly regarded by top universities worldwide. A standout addition to your college application portfolio that demonstrates analytical ability and financial literacy.

📊

Simulation Trading

Teams trade on a professional-grade paper trading platform, making real-time decisions in live market conditions. Experience the pressure and thrill of actual markets.

📝

Research Report

Each team submits a comprehensive investment strategy report — demonstrating analytical rigor, market insight, and the ability to articulate a coherent thesis.

How It Works

Competition Format

01

Form Teams

Assemble a team of 4–6 members. Assign roles: analysts, traders, and report writers.

02

Research

Conduct deep market research. Build your thesis, identify sectors, and select positions.

03

Trade

Execute trades on the simulation platform throughout the competition window.

04

Report

Submit your investment strategy report. Articulate your reasoning, risk management, and results.

Team Format

Build Your Crew

Teams of 4 to 6 members collaborate throughout the competition. Each member contributes to trading decisions and the final research report.

Cross-functional roles are encouraged — analysts who dig into fundamentals, traders who time entries, and writers who craft compelling reports. Every member plays a critical part.
